# test008 Charter

Status: active

This charter is the project's shared compass. Tars uses it to help the team stay aligned in discussion and work.

## Goals

<!-- tars-charter-area: goals -->
Plan out a holiday with the family of 4 to New Zealand. We would like to visit the South Island and travel by car around the island ending in Queenstown. we fly into Wellington and then fly out of Queenstown. We line in Perth. The kids are 9 and 11.

## Values

<!-- tars-charter-area: values -->
- **Enjoyment for all four travellers:** Choose experiences that engage both adults and children aged 9 and 11.
- **A comfortable family pace:** Protect time for rest, flexibility, and unhurried enjoyment rather than treating the holiday as a checklist.
- **Meaningful South Island experiences:** Prioritise the landscapes, wildlife, towns, and activities that make the South Island distinctive.
- **A smooth end-to-end journey:** Make the route from Wellington to the South Island and onward to Queenstown feel coherent and manageable.

## Principles

<!-- tars-charter-area: principles -->
- Keep daily driving distances realistic for a family with children, and avoid scheduling long driving days back-to-back.
- Build the itinerary toward Queenstown without unnecessary backtracking.
- Balance outdoor sights and scenic drives with hands-on, child-friendly activities and downtime.
- Allow buffer time for weather, road conditions, and spontaneous stops.
- Treat Wellington-to-South-Island transport, car hire, and any ferry or domestic flight as one connected itinerary when comparing options.
