### Acceptance criteria

- Start the journey in Wellington, include travel through the South Island by car, reach Queenstown on 22 September, and finish with departure from Queenstown on 2 October.

- Address Wellington-to-South-Island transport and car-hire logistics as part of the connected route.
- Clearly identify assumptions and details still requiring confirmation, such as flight times, accommodation, budget, transport bookings, and activity availability.
- Format the result as a readable Markdown itinerary document.
